# Design Context

## Target Audience
Small group — me + a few friends/athletes. Self-explanatory but power-user focused. No heavy hand-holding needed.

## Brand Personality
**Focused / clean but warm.** Clear information hierarchy with breathing room. Feels considered, not stark. Not decorative. Numbers are the hero — the UI gets out of the way.

## Stack
Next.js 15 App Router, TailwindCSS, shadcn/ui (v4), black/white/violet theme with dark mode (`darkMode: 'class'`).

## Design Principles
- **Data first**: Numbers and progress are the point. Decoration serves nothing.
- **One primary action per view**: Reduce choice. The most common action should be obvious.
- **Consistent rhythm**: Same heading scale, same card patterns, same spacing throughout.
- **Navigation belongs in the nav**: Don't repeat navigation patterns inside page content.
